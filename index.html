Features

Everything between “it’s done” and “it’s live”

The pre-release window is where launches are won or quietly go wrong. pre re gives it a home.

Staged releases

Every release lives in its own lane until you promote it. Nothing leaks by accident.

Instant previews

A shareable link the moment you push. No spin-up, no waiting, no stale screenshots.

Diff-aware review

How it works

Three steps, then it’s live

  1. 01

    Connect your project

    Point pre re at a repo or a deployed URL. It picks up your branches as release candidates.

  2. 02

    Stage the release

    Preview it privately, gather sign-off, and watch the checklist close itself out.

  3. 03

    Promote when ready

    One click publishes. One click rolls it back. No re-deploy, no re-negotiating.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

What exactly is pre re?

A workspace for the stretch between finishing work and shipping it. You stage a release, preview it privately, collect the sign-offs, then promote it when you decide.

Is the pre-release usable today?

Yes — v0.1.0 is live for invited teams while the surface settles. Request access and you’ll be added in the order requests arrive.

Where does my code live?

Where it already lives. pre re reads what it needs to build a preview and keeps release metadata in your workspace — your repo stays the source of truth.

What happens when it leaves pre-release?

Nothing breaks. Releases become generally available, preview links keep their expiry, and the API is versioned from day one.
